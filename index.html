TI4 Balanced Map Generator by Barci + AI

Balanced maps for 3–6 players · layouts & tile data from the TI4 Map Lab

Always active: at most 1 wormhole per player slice and no wormhole directly at a home system.

Secures each faction's beneficial feature within reach (≤2 tiles): Muaat→supernova, Saar→asteroid field, Empyrean→nebula, Vuil'raith→gravity rift, Creuss→α/β wormhole.
The option “reachable for neighbours” places it in the contested border zone if possible: own + both neighbours, otherwise own + one neighbour (the achieved level is shown below the map). Each faction can be selected only once. Discordant Stars factions are not included.

What happens when you click “Generate map”?
  1. Draw a tile pool (blue/red target, wormholes in pairs).
  2. Repeat: random map → optimisation across all five evaluations (Simple Slice, Joebrew, Resource, Influence and Tech Access) → bank swaps, with “kicks” in between.
  3. If anything is still one-sided, the map is regenerated with a fresh tile pool.
  4. Stop as soon as no evaluation is above 2 % (typically 4–10 s).

Legal placement (no two anomalies or identical wormholes adjacent) and wormhole pairs are always preserved. The result below the map shows the gap per evaluation and any one-sided result.

With “Consider factions”, each faction's beneficial feature is also placed in its own area (Muaat→supernova, Saar→asteroid field, Empyrean→nebula, Vuil'raith→gravity rift, Creuss→α/β wormhole) and fixed during optimisation. With “reachable for neighbours”, it is placed on a tile the neighbours can reach as well (contested border zone).
